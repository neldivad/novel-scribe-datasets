## Scene

**Ann Jee Nier:** It didn't happen in the system. It happened in the building. To the servers. At 1am. To me.

**Mae Nij Er:** Could you write that up as a win? A one-liner I can push up to See Eff Oh's people. "Proactively prevented Q3 checkout outage." Something punchy.
