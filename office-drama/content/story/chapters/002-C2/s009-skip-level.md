## Beat
Ann takes a recruiter's call — and stops talking herself out of leaving.

## Scene

**Ms Poe Cher:** Ann! Thanks for picking up, I'll be quick, I know you're heads-down. We're building out reliability at Halcyon and your name came up. Twice. Unprompted.
**Ann Jee Nier:** People say my name and then rank me last. It's a whole thing here.
**Ms Poe Cher:** ...They ranked you last?
**Ann Jee Nier:** Bottom decile. 18.40. Apparently if you prevent the fire instead of fighting it, the fire never happened, so neither did you.
**Ms Poe Cher:** That is the single most "company about to lose its best engineer" sentence I have ever heard.

---

**Ann Jee Nier:** I've said no to three of these calls this quarter. Out of — I don't know. Loyalty to a building.
**Ms Poe Cher:** And this call?
**Ann Jee Nier:** This call I'm still on.
**Ms Poe Cher:** That's new.
**Ann Jee Nier:** It's new.

---

**Ms Poe Cher:** Send me your no-meeting days. We'll talk real numbers — the kind that go up when you keep things from breaking.
**Ann Jee Nier:** ...Send the invite. I'll probably take it.
**Ms Poe Cher:** The meeting, or the job?
**Ann Jee Nier:** Ask me again after the meeting.
