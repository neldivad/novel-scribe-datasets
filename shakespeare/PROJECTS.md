# Shakespeare → NVS projects

Public-domain plays from Project Gutenberg. One project per play; ACTs are chapters, SCENEs are scene.md.
Run from the repository root:

```bash
scribe ingest-nvs --path shakespeare/a-midsummer-nights-dream --work-id shakespeare-a-midsummer-nights-dream   # A Midsummer Night’s Dream — 5 acts, 9 scenes, 29 chars
scribe ingest-nvs --path shakespeare/hamlet --work-id shakespeare-hamlet   # Hamlet — 5 acts, 20 scenes, 35 chars
scribe ingest-nvs --path shakespeare/julius-caesar --work-id shakespeare-julius-caesar   # Julius Caesar — 5 acts, 18 scenes, 47 chars
scribe ingest-nvs --path shakespeare/king-lear --work-id shakespeare-king-lear   # King Lear — 5 acts, 26 scenes, 26 chars
scribe ingest-nvs --path shakespeare/macbeth --work-id shakespeare-macbeth   # Macbeth — 5 acts, 28 scenes, 39 chars
scribe ingest-nvs --path shakespeare/much-ado-about-nothing --work-id shakespeare-much-ado-about-nothing   # Much Ado About Nothing — 5 acts, 13 scenes, 24 chars
scribe ingest-nvs --path shakespeare/othello --work-id shakespeare-othello   # Othello — 5 acts, 15 scenes, 24 chars
scribe ingest-nvs --path shakespeare/romeo-and-juliet --work-id shakespeare-romeo-and-juliet   # Romeo and Juliet — 5 acts, 24 scenes, 32 chars
scribe ingest-nvs --path shakespeare/the-merchant-of-venice --work-id shakespeare-the-merchant-of-venice   # The Merchant of Venice — 5 acts, 20 scenes, 25 chars
scribe ingest-nvs --path shakespeare/twelfth-night --work-id shakespeare-twelfth-night   # Twelfth Night — 5 acts, 18 scenes, 19 chars
```
